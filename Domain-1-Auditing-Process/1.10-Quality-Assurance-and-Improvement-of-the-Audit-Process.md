# 1.10 Quality Assurance and Improvement of the Audit Process

> **Markers:** 📓 = in my Google Doc notes · ⭐ = frequently tested · ➕ = not in the review manual, verify · unmarked = gap to study. Numbering follows the ISACA CISA Review Manual 28th Ed.

- Audit process itself must improve continuously
### 1.10.1 Audit committee oversight 📓
- Independent oversight for fair, thorough audits 📓
- Oversees IS audit function and chief audit executive interaction
- Without committee, a designated group or individual oversees
- Continuous performance monitoring via periodic reporting
### 1.10.2 Audit quality assurance (QA) 📓
- Needs executive management commitment and support 📓⭐
- Provides a structured framework to improve effectiveness 📓
- Success shown by continuously improving KPIs 📓
- Issue closure rate: management responsiveness 📓
- Audit leadership and project leads own quality
- Documented procedures: manuals, wikis, sampling guidance
### 1.10.3 Audit team training and development 📓
- Continuous education addresses emerging risks 📓
- Without it, skills become outdated 📓
- Best indicator: expanded audit coverage of new areas 📓
- Formal development plan for all members
- Training and certifications by role
- Budget supports training needs
### 1.10.4 Monitoring
- Quality Management System (QMS) standardizes processes 📓
- Accreditation of the audit function improves reliability of audit outcomes 📓⭐
- Monitoring initiatives
  - Audit QA: review results, track remediation
  - Independence monitoring: self-report impairments
  - Certification and accreditations: assigned ownership
  - Continued professional education monitoring
- Leadership reviews work papers and final deliverables
- Formal review process by risk (detail review, engagement quality review, QA)
