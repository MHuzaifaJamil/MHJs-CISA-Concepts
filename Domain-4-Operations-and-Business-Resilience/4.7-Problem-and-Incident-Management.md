# 4.7 Problem and Incident Management

> **Markers:** 📓 = in my Google Doc notes · ⭐ = frequently tested · ➕ = not in the review manual, verify · unmarked = gap to study. Numbering follows the ISACA CISA Review Manual 28th Ed.

- General controls over hardware, software, telecommunications, networks, applications, data
### 4.7.1 Problem management
- Investigates major or similar incidents to find root cause
- Root cause analysis methods
  - Fishbone/Ishikawa diagram
  - Brainstorming
  - 5 Whys
- Root cause identified becomes known error; workaround developed
- Known error database (KEDB)
- Objective: reduce number and severity of incidents
- Contrast: incident management restores service quickly
### 4.7.2 Process of incident handling
- Critical ITSM process; reactive; restores service per SLA
- Incident life cycle: initiation, classification, assignment to specialists, resolution, closure
- Prioritize by impact and urgency
- Unresolved incidents escalated per management criteria
- SLAs define acceptable incident statistics
### 4.7.3 Detection, documentation, control, resolution and reporting of abnormal conditions
- Automated or manual log of abnormal conditions
- Typical errors logged
  - Application, system, network, telecommunication, operator, hardware errors
- Error log entry items
  - Error date, code, description, source
  - Escalation date and time
  - Department responsible for resolution
  - Status code (open, closed pending date, irresolvable)
  - Resolution description and narrative
  - Initials: log maintainer, closer
- Anyone may add to log; updates restricted and traceable
- Separation of duties: closer differs from log maintainer/initiator
- Unresolved problems risk business interruption and corrupted data
- Escalation procedures
  - Contact details of specialists
  - Problems needing urgent resolution
  - Problems that can wait until working hours
- Auditor checks problems assigned to capable owners and resolved on time
### 4.7.4 Support / help desk
- Technical support: specialist production knowledge, change support, inform management of technology
- First, single, central point of contact for users
- Triage then escalate by complexity and expertise
- Document all incidents; escalate per management priorities
- Typical support functions
  - Determine incident source; corrective action
  - Initiate problem reports
  - Detailed network, system, application knowledge
  - Second- and third-tier support
  - Vendor software documentation and releases
  - Signal abnormal call patterns to IS operations
  - Answer inquiries about specific systems
  - Technical support for computerized telecommunications processing
  - Maintain documentation of in-house utilities and systems
### 4.7.5 Network management tools
- Protocol analyzer: captures and records real-time packet data 📓⭐
- Response time reports
- Downtime reports: remedies include SDN to bundle connectivity
- Help desk reports: history of problems and resolutions
- Online monitors: echo checking, status checking
- Network monitors: real-time node status
- Network (protocol) analyzers: data link/network level, hardware-based
- SNMP
  - Agents on each device; console polls
  - Use SNMPv2 or v3 for encryption
- ICMP
  - Error reporting for unreachable hosts
  - Does not exchange data; part of TCP/IP stack
### 4.7.6 Problem management reporting reviews
- Best incident-program indicator: incident resolution mean time 📓⭐
- Areas reviewed
  - Interviews with IS operations personnel
  - Performance records
  - Outstanding error log entries
  - Help desk call logs
  - Operations documentation and IT procedures
- Questions
  - Documented procedures for logging, analyzing, resolving, escalating
  - Valid reasons for processing delays
  - Recurring problems identified and prevented
  - Recurring problems not reported to IS management
  - Statistics on online processing performance adequate
