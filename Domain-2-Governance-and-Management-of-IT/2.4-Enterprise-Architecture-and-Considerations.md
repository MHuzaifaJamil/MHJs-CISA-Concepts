# 2.4 Enterprise Architecture and Considerations

> **Markers:** 📓 = in my Google Doc notes · ⭐ = frequently tested · ➕ = not in the review manual, verify · unmarked = gap to study. Numbering follows the ISACA CISA Review Manual 28th Ed.

- EA documents IT assets in a structured manner
- Supports understanding, management and planning of IT investment
- Auditor follows chosen EA as main information source; checks systems align
- Zachman ➕
  - First published by John Zachman, late 1980s
  - Analogy: building construction, abstract to physical
  - Matrix columns: data, function, network, people, process, strategy
  - Matrix rows: scope, enterprise model, systems model, technology model, detailed representation
  - Goal: complete all cells
  - Technology perspective: guides technology choices (Java EE, .NET, ERP, virtualization, cloud)
  - Business process perspective: map process model to upper rows
- TOGAF ➕
  - The Open Group framework for designing, planning, implementing, governing enterprise IT architecture
- SABSA ➕
  - Sherwood Applied Business Security Architecture
  - Focuses on information security architecture
- EA aligns IT and business goals 📓
