# 2.1 Laws, Regulations and Industry Standards

> **Markers:** 📓 = in my Google Doc notes · ⭐ = frequently tested · ➕ = not in the review manual, verify · unmarked = gap to study. Numbering follows the ISACA CISA Review Manual 28th Ed.

- Governance must consider laws, regulations, industry standards of location and industry
- Laws affect data life cycle: receipt, processing, storage, transmission, distribution, destruction
- Laws evolve; IS auditor must stay current
- Globally recognized compliance drivers
  - Privacy and confidentiality of personal data
  - Intellectual property rights
  - Reliability of financial information
- Specific legislation is not tested on CISA exam
### 2.1.1 Impact of laws, regulations and standards on IS audit
- Statutory and regulatory requirements 📓
- Terms used interchangeably: GEIT, EGIT, governance of IT, IT governance (ITG)
- Audits generally ascertain compliance with statutory requirements
- Multi-jurisdiction enterprises must know each jurisdiction's requirements
- Laws can apply outside the home jurisdiction
  - GDPR applies to anyone handling EU individuals' data
#### IIA considerations when auditing regulatory compliance
- Standards and procedures established to reduce criminal activity risk
- Responsibility assigned to senior personnel
- Reliable staff: background checks before authority roles
- Communication of procedures via training or documentation
- Compliance monitoring and auditing
- Consistent enforcement with disciplinary action
- Appropriate response to offense and prevention of repeats
#### UNCTAD Global Cyberlaw Tracker
- First global mapping of cyberlaws
- Tracks e-transactions, consumer protection, data protection/privacy, cybercrime
- Covers 194 UNCTAD member states
### 2.1.2 Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC)
- Assurance processes cannot exist in silos
- Governance: managing policies, processes and decisions
- Risk management: identifying, assessing and treating risk
- Compliance: adhering to laws, regulations, standards, policies
- OCEG coined GRC: reliably achieve objectives, address uncertainty, act with integrity
- Assurance functions: governance, internal audit, compliance, ERM, operational risk, incident management
- Usually focused on financial, IT and legal areas
  - Financial GRC: proper financial processes, SOX compliance
  - IT GRC: proper IT operation and policy compliance
  - Legal GRC: overall regulatory compliance for location and industry
- Goal: holistic view of risk blocking objectives
### 2.1.3 Specific laws and standards (reference)
#### SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley Act 2002) 📓
- Protects investors from fraudulent financial reporting 📓
- Section 302: officers certify reports 📓
- Section 404: internal control assessment 📓
- Applies to US public companies 📓
- Auditors assess IT general controls over financial systems 📓
- Senior management signs off on adequacy of internal controls
- Financial GRC example
#### Privacy and data laws
- GDPR ➕
  - EU law, applies globally to enterprises dealing with EU individuals
  - Legal basis includes consent, legitimate interest
- HIPAA ➕
- PCI DSS ➕
#### Other laws listed in ORM
- US: GLBA, FERPA, COPPA, CIPA, FISMA 2002
- Canada: PIPEDA
- South Korea: PIPA
- Japan: Financial Instruments and Exchange Act (FIEA) 2006
- South Africa: POPI Act
- UK: Ministry of Defence DEFCON 658, UK Data Protection Act
- Saudi Arabia: PDPL
- Australia: Privacy Act 1988
- Basel Accords (regulatory driver for IT controls)
