# 2.11 Assurance and Quality Management of IT

> **Markers:** 📓 = in my Google Doc notes · ⭐ = frequently tested · ➕ = not in the review manual, verify · unmarked = gap to study. Numbering follows the ISACA CISA Review Manual 28th Ed.

- QA program: planned, systematic pattern of actions giving confidence of conformance
- Integrity and reliability of IT processes depend on QA
### 2.11.1 Quality assurance
- QA verifies changes authorized, tested, implemented in controlled manner
- Oversees developer segregation from production, version maintenance, source code integrity
- Quality assurance (QA)
  - Ensures personnel follow prescribed quality processes
  - Example: sets ISO 9001-compliant procedures
  - Develops, promulgates, maintains IT standards; trains on them
- Quality control (QC)
  - Observation techniques and activities to meet quality requirements
  - Tests and reviews to verify defect-free software before production
  - Checks programs and documentation follow standards and naming conventions
  - Periodically checks input, processing, output accuracy
- QA should be independent
- Small enterprises: individuals may hold multiple roles
- Never review own work; avoid SoD conflicts (e.g., DBA reviewing database-impacting changes)
### 2.11.2 Quality management
- How IT processes are controlled, measured, improved
- Areas of control
  - Software development, maintenance, implementation
  - Hardware and software acquisition
  - Day-to-day operations
  - IT control performance
  - Service management
  - Security
  - HR management
  - General administration
- Documented processes are evidence of effective governance
- Quality standards give predictable, measurable, repeatable, certified environment
### 2.11.3 Operational excellence
- Teams improve efficiency and effectiveness of operations
- Eliminate waste, streamline processes, improve collaboration
- Use data and analytics to find improvements
- Common responsibilities
  - Develop and implement best practices
  - Train and support employees
  - Conduct research and development
  - Manage knowledge and information
  - Serve as resource for employees and stakeholders
### Case study (ORM)
- Case: Accenco, small fast-growing financial institution
- Risk committee met three times in two years, no minutes
- IT strategic initiatives missing from budget
- No full-time CIO; IS manager reports to CFO
- Programmers need only DBA approval for production data access
- Programmers give code to librarian for migration
- Internal audit reports to CFO
