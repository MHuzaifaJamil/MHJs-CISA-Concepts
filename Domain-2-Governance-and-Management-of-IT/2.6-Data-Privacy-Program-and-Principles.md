# 2.6 Data Privacy Program and Principles

> **Markers:** 📓 = in my Google Doc notes · ⭐ = frequently tested · ➕ = not in the review manual, verify · unmarked = gap to study. Numbering follows the ISACA CISA Review Manual 28th Ed.

- Privacy governance program identifies and manages all personal information
- Personal data: information relating to an identifiable person
- Controls protect privacy across full life cycle
#### Privacy management practice areas
- Privacy roles and responsibilities
- Training and awareness
- Vendor and third-party monitoring
- Privacy audit process
- Privacy incident management
#### Program requirements (figure 2.12)
- Internal requirements
  - Assign privacy roles
  - Define and inventory personal information
  - Employee privacy policies, procedures, training
  - Vendor management programs
  - Manage privacy incidents
  - Privacy audits and assessments
  - Harmonize with organizational culture
  - Account for system capabilities and limits
- External requirements
  - Identify applicable laws, regulations, standards, contractual obligations
  - Choose privacy principles and frameworks
  - Post privacy notice reflecting actual practice
  - Third-party oversight
  - Address data subject rights
  - Customer, investor, industry requirements
  - Existing contractual relationships
#### Privacy principles and laws
- ISO/TS 17975:2022 consent for personal health information
- OECD Privacy Principles
- AICPA Generally Accepted Privacy Principles
- ISACA Privacy Principles
- EU GDPR and Trans-Atlantic Data Privacy Framework (DPF)
### 2.6.1 Privacy documentation
- Demonstrates practices, meets obligations, builds trust, shows due care
- Policies, notices and documents align with laws, contracts, standards
- Inward and outward documents reviewed regularly
- Program breadth and depth meets or exceeds requirements
- Types of documentation
  - Privacy notice
    - Outward-facing statement for data subjects and authorities
    - Describes collection, use, retention, safeguarding, disclosure
    - Establishes legal accountability
    - Controller and processor must comply with it
    - Explains what, why, how used, who shared with, retention, impact, destruction
    - Explains how to exercise rights; supports consent
    - Formats: web page, forms, brochures, facility documents, contracts, CCTV signs
    - Keep separate from terms of use
    - Allow sufficient time between notice and collection
    - Internal version is privacy policy or statement
  - Consent form
    - Documents agreement to privacy notice
    - Forms: opt-in/opt-out, tick box, signature box
    - Provisions: consent for existing data, renewal if noncompliant
    - Informed consent is ongoing exchange, not just signature
    - Data subjects can withdraw consent; make it easy
  - Personal information inventory
    - Repository of personal data assets collected, derived, processed, stored
    - Internal document, not a regulatory requirement itself
    - Include data flow mappings
    - Keep current
    - Covers network, applications, systems, storage, physical forms
  - Other documentation
    - Activity logs (training attendance, failed identity verifications, access)
    - Data protection legal requirements document
    - Privacy risk assessment reports
    - Privacy impact assessment (PIA) reports
    - Privacy governance reports
      - Show current compliance, improvements, incidents, program status
      - Demonstrate value, raise awareness, communicate successes, engage stakeholders
    - Training activity records (topic, attendees, date, delivery, test results)
    - Data incident register (facts, effects, remedial and preventive measures)
    - Individual rights register (requests, how and when received)
### 2.6.2 Audit process (privacy)
- Audits, assessments, testing, compliance reviews
- Annual data protection audits aid compliance
- Can identify architecture failures to support privacy by design
- Supports ISACA Privacy Principle 9: Monitoring, Measuring and Reporting
- Framework to audit, measure and monitor
  - Effectiveness of privacy program
  - Compliance with policies, standards, laws
  - Use of privacy tools
  - Privacy-enhancing technology advances
  - Changes in laws
  - Types and numbers of breaches
  - Privacy risk areas in digital ecosystem
  - Third parties with access and risk levels
- Report compliance to stakeholders
- Integrate international privacy practices and check in audits
- Procedures for personal data use in investigations, monitoring, analytics
- Anonymize data where law forbids monitoring raw personal data; audit effectiveness
#### IS auditor evaluation points
- Privacy requirements in third-party contracts
- Retention and destruction policies implemented
- Cross-border regulations addressed
- Employees trained on privacy regulations
- Data inventory created and maintained
- Data subject requests handled appropriately
### 2.6.3 Privacy-enhancing techniques ➕
- Pseudonymisation 📓
  - Replaces identifiers with a token 📓
  - Mapping table must be strongly protected 📓
  - Not a substitute for encryption and access controls 📓
- Anonymisation
  - Used when law prohibits monitoring raw personal data
