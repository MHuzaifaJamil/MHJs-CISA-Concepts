# 3.8 Postimplementation Review

> **Markers:** 📓 = in my Google Doc notes · ⭐ = frequently tested · ➕ = not in the review manual, verify · unmarked = gap to study. Numbering follows the ISACA CISA Review Manual 28th Ed.

- Validate control design and technical integrity 📓⭐
- Formal closure: results information, improve future projects, orderly resource release
- Closure determines objectives met or excused; identify lessons learned
- Review typically weeks or months after completion
- Part of benefits realization; estimates overall success and business impact
- Determines appropriate controls built in
#### Review scope
- Adequacy: meets user requirements and business objectives; controls defined and implemented
- Projected cost versus benefits or ROI measurements
- Recommendations and plan to implement them
- Development process: methodologies, standards, techniques followed
- Development process: appropriate PM techniques used
- Risk of operating system within acceptable levels
- Second joint review by development team and users after sufficient production time
#### Project closeout steps (figure 3.28)
- Step 1: assign outstanding issues to individuals with budget
- Step 2: assign custody of contracts; archive or pass on documentation
- Step 3: postimplementation review with team, users, stakeholders
- Step 3 content criteria: deliverable targets, incentives, schedule and costs
- Step 3 process criteria: team dynamics, internal communication, external stakeholder relationships
- Step 4: document risks and update risk register
- Step 5: second postimplementation review after benefits and costs realized
#### Effective review requirements
- Identify information to review during feasibility and design
- Collect at each stage; checkpoints on process effectiveness and estimate accuracy
- Establish business measurements before project and after implementation
- Allow enough business cycles to realize actual ROI
#### Measurements of critical success factors (figure 3.29)
- Productivity: transactions per month, transactions per user
- Quality: number of discrepancies, disputes
- Quality: occurrences of fraud or misuse detection
- Economic value: processing time reduction, administration cost value
- Customer service: turnaround time for questions
- Customer service: frequency of useful communication to users
#### Who performs review
- Joint project team and end users: critique process
- Independent group (internal or external audit): measures business value
### 3.8.1 IS auditor's role in postimplementation review
- Auditor independent of system development
- Auditor who consulted on development should not perform review
- Focuses on control aspects of development and implementation
- Document all audit involvement in work papers
- Reuse audit documentation during maintenance and changes
- Periodic review that system still meets objectives cost-effectively
#### Auditor functions
- Determine objectives and requirements achieved: user use, trouble tickets, work orders, satisfaction
- Confirm feasibility cost benefits measured, analyzed, reported
- Review program change requests for design or requirement problems
- Review controls operate as designed
- Use enterprise asset management (EAM) module to test key operations if included
- Review operators' error logs for resource or operating problems
- Review input and output control balances and reports
### Case study (ORM)
- Wonderwheels retailer: PCI-style cardholder protection readiness review
- Facts: WEP-encrypted wireless POS registers
- Facts: store POS servers send data over frame relay with strong encryption to HQ
- Facts: weekly sales data mailed on magnetic media to third party
- Facts: ERP not GDPR compliant
- Facts: database upgrade late, phased customization, pilot on real data
- Facts: database unpatched over two years; vendor support dropped
- Facts: internal audit excluded from key compliance decisions
- Facts: transactions hang and data corrupt; fixed errors recur
