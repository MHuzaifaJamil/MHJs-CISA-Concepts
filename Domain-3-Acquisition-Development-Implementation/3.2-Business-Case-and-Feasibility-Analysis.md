# 3.2 Business Case and Feasibility Analysis

> **Markers:** 📓 = in my Google Doc notes · ⭐ = frequently tested · ➕ = not in the review manual, verify · unmarked = gap to study. Numbering follows the ISACA CISA Review Manual 28th Ed.

- Business case gives information needed to decide whether project proceeds
- Answers why project should be undertaken or continued
- Key element of decisions throughout life cycle
- Stage gates or kill points: formal business case reviews
- If business case changes, reapprove through departmental planning and approval
- Feasibility study derives initial business case
#### Six elements of a feasibility study
- Project scope: business problem or opportunity, clear and concise
- Current analysis: understand current system, strengths, weaknesses
- Current analysis outcomes: working, minor changes, or upgrade/replacement
- Requirements: based on stakeholder needs and constraints
- Requirements examples: business, contractual, regulatory; user functional needs; technical attributes
- Approach: course of action, alternatives, rationale, build-versus-buy
- Evaluation: cost-effectiveness of selected approach
- Evaluation report: total cost, employee hours, material and facility, vendor costs
- Evaluation report: schedule start and end, cost-benefit analysis, ROI
- Review: formal review validates completeness; approve or reject
- Key stakeholders sign approved study
- Rejection rationale attached as lessons learned
### 3.2.1 IS auditor's role in business case development
- Understand how business defines business cases and ROI
- Missing ROI objectives suggests weak development approach
- Ensure feasibility process and decisions effective and unbiased
#### Reviewing the feasibility study
- Review criticality of business and IS process requirements
- Check whether existing systems can solve it; review alternatives
- Judge reasonableness of chosen solution
- Verify cost justifications and benefits are verifiable
- Review documentation for reasonableness
#### Requirements definition review
- Obtain requirements document; verify via user interviews
- Verify all affected user groups represented
- Verify management approval of initiation and cost
- Review conceptual design (transforms, data descriptions) against user needs
- Review conceptual design for control specifications
- Check reasonable number of vendors received proposal
- Review UAT specifications
- Assess candidacy for embedded audit routine; request in conceptual design
